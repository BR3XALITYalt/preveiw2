How it works. The video is processed in 10-second batches by FFmpeg running inside WebAssembly, entirely in your browser. The Mari Group and Not Scary treatments are native-filter approximations of the named effects. Pitch changes by track: none, +1 semitone, -2 semitones, +1 semitone.

    
Limitations. Browser FFmpeg is single-threaded and keeps everything in memory. Long videos or files over about 100 MB may fail or run very slowly. For heavy work, run the desktop FFmpeg version instead.

    
First load. The FFmpeg core is about 25 MB and is fetched from jsDelivr on the first run, then cached by the browser. Subsequent runs are fast.
